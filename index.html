Healed vs fresh

Judge it healed,
not just fresh.

Fresh tattoos can look darker, shinier and slightly swollen. The final result settles after healing.

Fresh work

Immediately after the appointment, colour and black ink may appear more saturated.

  • Colour can look especially bright
  • Surface shine may be present
  • Mild redness or swelling is normal
→

Healed work

Once healed, the tattoo shows its true contrast, softness and colour balance.

  • Black & gray settles into softer tones
  • Colour becomes more natural
  • Linework and shading are easier to judge
